## Project Overview

Olist Commerce Intelligence is an end-to-end e-commerce analytics project developed using the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset. The project combines SQL-based data analysis in BigQuery, advanced analytics in Python, and an AI-powered interactive dashboard to uncover insights related to customer behavior, revenue performance, operational efficiency, and business growth opportunities.

The objective of this project was to simulate a real-world business analytics engagement by transforming raw transactional data into actionable business recommendations through a structured analytics workflow.

---

## Business Objectives

- Analyze revenue growth and seasonality trends
- Segment customers using RFM analysis
- Measure customer retention through cohort analysis
- Evaluate payment method performance
- Assess delivery efficiency and customer satisfaction
- Identify top-performing products and categories
- Generate strategic business recommendations

## SQL Analysis

### 1. Revenue Trend Analysis
Analyzed monthly revenue, orders, average order value and seasonality trends.

### 2. RFM Customer Segmentation
Segmented customers into Champions, Loyal, At-Risk, and Lost groups based on Recency, Frequency and Monetary Value.

### 3. Cohort Retention Analysis
Tracked customer retention by acquisition cohort and measured repeat purchasing behavior.

### 4. Payment Method Analysis
Analyzed credit card, boleto, voucher and debit card performance across revenue and order metrics.

### 5. Delivery Performance Analysis
Measured actual vs estimated delivery times and evaluated their impact on customer satisfaction.

### 6. Geographic Analysis
Identified top-performing states and evaluated regional revenue distribution.

### 7. Product & Category Performance
Analyzed category-level revenue contribution and top-selling products.

## Key Insights

- A relatively small percentage of customers generate the majority of revenue.
- Customer retention declines significantly after the initial purchase period.
- Revenue demonstrates seasonal fluctuations across the year.
- Delivery delays negatively affect customer review scores.
- A small number of product categories account for a large share of revenue.
